会员附送额度

已开通的有效付费会员每月享有 140 次共用额度。打字发送用 1 次；发送录音合共用 2 次：自动转文字 1 次，聊天发送 1 次。也可先看文字，再自行发送，合共同样是 2 次。录音和回听不扣次数，AI 的文字回复不另计。

附送额度每月 1 日按马来西亚时间更新，未用完部分不累积。清空聊天或换设备不会恢复已用次数。

目前不提供付费加额服务。额度用完后，你仍可按会员权限聆听音疗，也可以继续每日打卡，等下个月的新额度再来聊聊。

Included with membership

Activated, eligible paid members receive 140 shared allowance units each month. A typed send uses 1 unit. Sending a voice note uses 2: 1 for automatic transcription and 1 for the chat send. Optional text preview followed by a later send also uses 2 in total. Recording and playback use no allowance; the AI text reply is not counted separately.

Included allowance renews on the 1st of each month in Malaysia time and does not roll over. Clearing chat or changing devices does not restore used units.

Paid top-ups are not offered. Once your chat allowance is used, you can keep listening within your membership and continue daily check-ins, then return for next month's allowance.
