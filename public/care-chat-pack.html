加额包使用说明

  1. 自由选择：每月已有一份聊天额度，用完后可等下个月，也可在付款功能开放后自行购买。不会自动购买、续购或更改会员费用。
  2. 付款前先看清楚：每份价格 RM10。正式开放时，结账前会显示可获得的文字发送次数、应付总额及适用条款；当前不接受订单。
  3. 跟随你的聊天账户：购买额度只加入付款订单对应的已验证账户。请使用你在 FC 的同一个邮箱；不能转给另一个账户，也不作现金余额使用。
  4. 先用每月额度：系统先使用当月包含的额度，再使用已购买额度。每月额度按马来西亚时间于每月一日更新，未用完部分不累积；已购买的未用额度不随月份更新而清零。
  5. 怎样计算使用：发送一条文字消息使用一次额度，AI 回复不另计。语音输入开放时，录音和回听不扣次数；按「转成文字」使用一次，确认发送文字再使用一次，与打字共用额度。清空聊天或换设备不会恢复已用次数。网络中断或回复获取失败时，若处理已经开始，仍可能计入一次；请勿连续重发，有疑问可联系 FC 查核。
  6. 付款与到账：只有付款平台确认成功后才加入额度，同一笔成功付款只加一次。如已扣款却未到账，请保留订单编号并联系 FC，不必再次付款。订单异常及退款安排将在付款开放前列明，不影响适用的消费者权利。
  7. 服务范围：加额包仅用于暖心聊聊，不会开通或延长音轨会员权限。聊天提供日常身心支持，不能用于紧急求助、医疗诊断或保证治疗效果。

Extra Chat Pack details

  1. Your choice: A chat allowance is included each month. When it runs out, wait for the next month or choose a pack once purchasing opens. There are no automatic purchases or renewals, and your membership price is unchanged.
  2. Clear details before payment: Each pack costs RM10. Before checkout opens, the number of text sends, total payable and applicable terms will be shown. Orders are not accepted yet.
  3. Linked to your chat account: Purchased allowance goes only to the verified account on the order. Use the same email as FC. Allowance cannot be transferred to another account or used as a cash balance.
  4. Included allowance first: Monthly allowance is used before purchased allowance. Included allowance renews on the first day of each month in Malaysia time and does not roll over. Unused purchased allowance is not cleared at the monthly reset.
  5. How use is counted: Sending a text message uses one allowance unit; the AI reply is not counted separately. When voice input is available, recording and playback use no units. Converting a recording uses one unit; sending the reviewed text uses another, from the same allowance. Clearing chat or changing devices does not restore used units. If processing began, a network interruption or missing reply may still use a unit. Avoid repeated resends and contact FC if you need a usage check.
  6. Payment and credit: Allowance is added only after payment is confirmed by the payment provider, once per successful payment. If charged without receiving allowance, keep your order reference and contact FC rather than paying again. Payment-issue and refund arrangements will be provided before checkout opens, without affecting applicable consumer rights.
  7. Service scope: Packs apply only to Care Chat and do not grant or extend audio membership. Chat provides everyday wellbeing support, not emergency assistance, medical diagnosis or guaranteed treatment results.
