Customer service workflow

  1. Verify the original FC comment and its author. Record the canonical comment ID/link, not a new label invented for this request. Check the old testimonial ledger to prevent a second reward.
  2. Approve genuine original sharing once. The system adds exactly 10 points. Reject unverifiable or duplicate submissions with a clear nonmedical reason.
  3. For redemptions, confirm the member’s paid active status and actual stock/appointment availability. No public stock counts. No need for renewal, including free days.
  4. Points are already reserved. Confirm stock, arrange with the member, collect RM9 shipping if shipped, and then fulfil with evidence. For collection/services, shipping is RM0.
  5. For a free day, actually extend the correct FC audio access by one day first; enter the before/after final access dates and evidence. This screen does not edit LearnWorlds billing or enrolment.
  6. For an unavailable reward or cancellation, record the reason and cancel to return points. The member may choose another item. Never fulfil and cancel the same order.

Do not put addresses, health reports, card numbers or testimonial narratives in this ledger. Arrange address/payment details privately with the member. Existing check-in and legacy approved days remain separate.
